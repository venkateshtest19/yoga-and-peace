Medical & Fitness Disclaimer

Please read this important information regarding your health and safety before participating.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Not a Substitute for Medical Advice

The content, classes, and services provided by Yoga and Peace are for general educational and wellness purposes only. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

Consult Your Doctor First

Before beginning any new exercise program, including gentle yoga, it is highly recommended that you consult with your primary care physician. This is especially important if you:

  • Have a history of heart disease, high blood pressure, or stroke.
  • Suffer from osteoporosis, severe arthritis, or joint replacements.
  • Experience chronic dizziness, vertigo, or balance issues.
  • Are pregnant or have recently undergone surgery.

Assumption of Risk

Yoga involves physical movement, stretching, and balance exercises. By participating in our in-person classes or using our online video library, you acknowledge that you are voluntarily participating in these activities and assume all risk of injury to yourself.

While our certified senior yoga therapists are trained to provide safe, adaptive modifications, you are responsible for listening to your own body. If a movement causes pain or discomfort, please stop immediately and inform your instructor.

No Guarantees of Results

While many of our members experience significant improvements in mobility, sleep, and stress reduction, individual results will vary. Yoga and Peace does not guarantee specific health outcomes, as they depend on your unique physiology, consistency, and overall health profile.

Emergency Protocol

If you feel unwell, dizzy, or experience chest pain during a class, please stop your practice immediately. Our studio is equipped with first-aid kits and automated external defibrillators (AEDs), and our staff is trained in basic emergency response. However, you are responsible for communicating your physical limits to us.
