Medical & Fitness Disclaimer

Your safety is our highest priority. Please read this important information regarding your health before participating.

1. Not a Substitute for Professional Medical Advice

The content, classes, and services provided by Yoga and Peace are for general educational, wellness, and fitness purposes only. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read or experienced at our studio.

2. Consult Your Physician Before Starting

Before beginning any new exercise program, including our gentle yoga classes, it is highly recommended that you consult with your primary care physician. This is especially important if you:

  • Have a history of heart disease, high blood pressure, or stroke.
  • Suffer from osteoporosis, severe arthritis, or have had joint replacements.
  • Experience chronic dizziness, vertigo, or balance issues.
  • Are pregnant or have recently undergone surgery.
  • Take medications that may affect your heart rate or balance.

3. Assumption of Risk

Yoga involves physical movement, stretching, balancing, and breathing exercises. By participating in our in-person classes or using our online video library, you acknowledge that you are voluntarily participating in these activities and assume all risk of injury to yourself. While our certified senior yoga therapists are trained to provide safe, adaptive modifications, you are ultimately responsible for listening to your own body. If a movement causes pain, dizziness, or discomfort, please stop immediately and inform your instructor.

4. No Guarantees of Specific Results

While many of our members experience significant improvements in mobility, sleep, stress reduction, and joint pain, individual results will vary. Yoga and Peace does not guarantee specific health outcomes, as they depend on your unique physiology, consistency, overall health profile, and adherence to medical advice.

5. Emergency Protocol in the Studio

If you feel unwell, dizzy, short of breath, or experience chest pain during a class, please stop your practice immediately. Our studio is equipped with first-aid kits and automated external defibrillators (AEDs), and our staff is trained in basic emergency response. However, you are responsible for communicating your physical limits to us before class begins.

6. Online Content Disclaimer

Our online video library is intended for general wellness. When practicing at home, ensure you are in a safe, clear environment. We recommend having a sturdy chair or wall nearby for support, and we advise against practicing alone if you have severe balance concerns.
