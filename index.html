Welcome to Your New Sanctuary

At Yoga and Peace, we believe that wellness has no expiration date. Whether you're 55 or 95, your body still holds incredible potential for healing, strength, and serenity.

Imagine a space where you never feel rushed. A place where the chairs are comfortable, the floors are safe, and the people understand exactly what it means to navigate the unique rhythm of an aging body. That is what we have built for you.

Founded by certified senior yoga therapists, our studio is a sanctuary built around your unique needs. We understand that life brings changes — stiff joints, balance concerns, chronic pain — and we've designed every class to meet you exactly where you are today.

No flexibility required. No judgment. Just patient, knowledgeable guidance in a warm, supportive environment where every small victory is celebrated.

Why Choose Yoga and Peace?

We're not just another yoga studio. We're a community built specifically for the wisdom, bodies, and lives of older adults.

🎓

Certified Senior Yoga Therapists

Our instructors hold specialized certifications in senior yoga therapy and geriatric fitness. They understand arthritis, osteoporosis, and joint replacement — so you're always in safe, knowledgeable hands that honor your body's timeline.

🪑

Gentle & Adaptable Pace

Every pose can be modified. We offer chair yoga, restorative sessions, and slow-flow classes designed for limited mobility. Come as you are — we'll adapt the practice to fit your body, not the other way around.

🌿

Proven Health Benefits

Research shows gentle yoga reduces fall risk, eases chronic joint pain, improves sleep quality, and lowers blood pressure. Our seniors report feeling more energetic, balanced, and emotionally grounded within just a few weeks.

🤝

Warm, Connected Community

Loneliness is a real health risk for seniors — and we're here to change that. Our classes become friendships. Our studio becomes a second home. Join in-person or online, and find your people in Springfield.
