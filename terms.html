Terms of Service

The guidelines that help our studio run smoothly, safely, and peacefully for our entire community.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using the Yoga and Peace website, signing up for a membership, or attending a class at our Springfield studio, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please refrain from using our services.

2. Membership and Billing

  • Monthly Billing: Memberships are billed automatically on a monthly cycle, on the anniversary of your sign-up date.
  • Cancellation: You may cancel your membership at any time. To avoid being charged for the upcoming month, cancellations must be submitted at least 24 hours before your next billing cycle.
  • Price Adjustments: We reserve the right to adjust membership pricing. If this occurs, we will provide all active members with at least 30 days' prior written notice via email.
  • Membership Freeze: If you are going on an extended vacation or recovering from an illness, you may request to "freeze" your membership for up to 30 days. Please contact us in advance to arrange this.

3. Studio Conduct and Community Guidelines

To ensure a peaceful, safe, and welcoming environment for our senior community, we ask that all members and guests adhere to the following:

  • Arrival Time: Please arrive 10 minutes early to your first class to complete any necessary paperwork and speak with the instructor about physical limitations.
  • Footwear: We practice barefoot or in clean, non-slip grip socks to maintain studio hygiene and prevent slipping.
  • Fragrances: Many seniors have respiratory sensitivities. We kindly ask that you refrain from wearing strong perfumes, colognes, or heavily scented lotions to class.
  • Electronics: Please keep mobile phones on silent and out of sight during classes to respect the mindfulness of others.
  • Respect: Treat our instructors, staff, fellow members, and studio property with kindness and respect. Harassment or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated.

4. Class Cancellations by the Studio

In the event of severe weather, instructor illness, or facility maintenance, Yoga and Peace may need to cancel classes. We will make every effort to notify you via email or phone as soon as possible. Missed classes due to studio cancellations will be credited to your account or added to your online video library access.
