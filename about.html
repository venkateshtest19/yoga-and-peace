Our Story & Mission

More than just a studio, we are a sanctuary built to honor your body, your pace, and your golden years.

Wellness Has No Expiration Date

We founded Yoga and Peace because we saw a gap. Most gyms are too loud, too fast, and too intimidating. Most yoga studios focus on extreme flexibility that many older adults feel they've lost. We created this space to bridge that gap.

At Yoga and Peace, we believe that your best years are still unfolding. Aging brings unique changes—stiffer joints, slower recovery, balance concerns—but it also brings wisdom, patience, and a deep desire for meaningful connection. You don't need to "push through the pain" to be healthy. You just need to move with intention.

Our studio is not a high-intensity boot camp. It is a calm, accessible sanctuary designed specifically for the 55+ community in Springfield. Here, you will never be rushed, judged, or pushed beyond your comfort zone. We meet you exactly where you are today, and we walk alongside you as you rediscover your strength.

The Yoga and Peace Approach

We blend ancient mindfulness with modern geriatric science to create practices that are safe, effective, and deeply comforting.

🩺

Science-Backed Safety

Every sequence is reviewed by geriatric physical therapists. We focus on joint lubrication, bone density, and fall prevention without risking injury. Your safety is our absolute first priority.

🪑

100% Adaptable

Chair yoga, wall support, bolsters, and blankets are standard in every class. If a pose doesn't feel right, we seamlessly modify it for you. Your pace is always the right pace.

🧠

Mind-Body Connection

We incorporate guided breathing and gentle meditation to lower cortisol, ease anxiety, and improve sleep quality. It's not just about stretching your muscles; it's about calming your mind.

We Don't Just Teach Poses, We Teach Peace of Mind

Our certified senior yoga therapists are trained to look beyond the physical. They notice when you're holding tension in your shoulders, when you're favoring one side, or when you just need a quiet moment to breathe.

Many of our members come to us carrying the weight of chronic pain, grief, or the frustration of a body that doesn't move like it used to. Our instructors hold space for those feelings. They offer gentle adjustments, encouraging words, and the profound reminder that showing up on the mat is enough.

We celebrate the small victories. Touching your toes isn't the goal; feeling a little less stiffness, sleeping a little deeper, and smiling a little more often—that is our true measure of success.

A Space That Cares for You

We've designed our physical studio in Springfield to eliminate barriers and maximize your peace of mind from the moment you walk in.

🚪 Accessible & Safe Entry

Zero-step entry and wide doorways accommodate walkers, canes, and wheelchairs with ease. No navigating steep stairs or heavy doors.

🛡️ Fall-Prevention Flooring

Our practice rooms feature premium non-slip flooring and ample, sturdy handrails. We also keep the lighting bright and even to prevent dizziness.

🌡️ Comfortable Climate

Aging joints hate the cold. We keep our studio comfortably warm year-round, and we provide plenty of cozy blankets and supportive chairs for resting.

🚻 Accessible Restrooms

Our facilities include spacious, accessible restrooms equipped with sturdy grab bars and emergency call buttons for your complete safety.
