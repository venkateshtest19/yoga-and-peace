Our Story & Mission

Creating a safe, nurturing sanctuary where older adults can move freely, breathe deeply, and thrive together.

Wellness Has No Expiration Date

Yoga and Peace was founded on a simple, powerful belief: your best years are still unfolding, and your body is capable of healing at any age.

Many seniors are told to "take it easy" or that they are "too old" to start exercising. We are here to gently challenge that narrative. Aging brings unique changes—stiffer joints, slower recovery, balance concerns—but it also brings wisdom, patience, and a deep desire for meaningful connection.

Our studio is not a high-intensity gym. It is a calm, accessible sanctuary designed specifically for the 55+ community. Here, you will never be rushed, judged, or pushed beyond your comfort zone. We meet you exactly where you are today, and we walk alongside you as you rediscover your strength.

The Yoga and Peace Approach

We blend ancient mindfulness with modern geriatric science to create practices that are safe, effective, and deeply comforting.

🩺

Science-Backed Safety

Every sequence is reviewed by geriatric physical therapists. We focus on joint lubrication, bone density, and fall prevention without risking injury.

🪑

100% Adaptable

Chair yoga, wall support, bolsters, and blankets are standard in every class. If a pose doesn't feel right, we seamlessly modify it for you.

🧠

Mind-Body Connection

We incorporate guided breathing and gentle meditation to lower cortisol, ease anxiety, and improve sleep quality—proven benefits for older adults.

A Studio Built for Your Comfort

We've designed our physical space to eliminate barriers and maximize peace of mind.

  • ✓ Zero-step entry and wide doorways for walkers and wheelchairs.
  • ✓ Non-slip flooring and ample handrails in all practice rooms.
  • ✓ Accessible restrooms with grab bars and emergency call buttons.
  • ✓ Comfortable climate control (we keep it warm for aging joints!).
  • ✓ Plenty of seating in our lobby for resting before or after class.
