Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are our top priorities. Here is how we handle your information.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Yoga and Peace, we only collect information that is necessary to provide you with our services and ensure your safety in our studio.

Personal Information

When you sign up for a class, create an account, or fill out our contact form, we may ask for your name, email address, phone number, and basic health or mobility information (such as joint replacements or balance concerns) to help our instructors keep you safe.

Payment Information

We use secure, third-party payment processors (like Stripe or Square) to handle your membership fees. We do not store your full credit card numbers on our own servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule your classes and manage your membership.
  • To communicate with you about studio updates, class cancellations, or wellness tips.
  • To customize your yoga experience based on your physical needs and health disclosures.
  • To process your payments securely.

3. Sharing Your Information

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We only share data with trusted service providers who help us run our studio (e.g., our email newsletter provider or payment processor), and they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies to ensure the site functions properly (like remembering your mobile menu preferences). We do not use invasive tracking cookies or sell your browsing data to advertisers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it at any time. Simply email us at hello@yogaandpeace.com and we will process your request within 30 days.
