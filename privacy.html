Privacy Policy

Your privacy, trust, and peace of mind are our top priorities. Here is exactly how we handle your information.

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Yoga and Peace, we believe that a true sanctuary extends to your digital privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being completely transparent about how we collect, use, and safeguard your data. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide you with our services, ensure your physical safety in our studio, and communicate with you effectively.

Personal Identification Information

When you sign up for a membership, book a class, or fill out our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.

Health and Mobility Information

To keep you safe during our physical classes, we may ask for basic health disclosures, such as joint replacements, balance concerns, or chronic conditions like arthritis. This information is kept strictly confidential and is only shared with our certified yoga therapists to adapt your practice.

Payment Information

We use secure, PCI-compliant third-party payment processors (such as Stripe or Square) to handle your membership fees. We do not store your full credit card numbers or sensitive financial data on our own servers.

Technical and Usage Data

When you visit our website, we automatically collect standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. We use this data solely to improve our website's performance and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule your classes, manage your membership, and process your payments securely.
  • To communicate with you about studio updates, class cancellations, schedule changes, or wellness tips.
  • To customize your yoga experience and ensure our instructors can provide safe, adaptive modifications for your specific physical needs.
  • To improve our website functionality and understand how our community interacts with our online resources.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We only share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our studio and website. These partners include our email newsletter provider, our payment processor, and our website hosting service (Vercel). All partners are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing their services to us.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies to ensure the site functions properly (such as remembering your mobile menu preferences). We also use Google Analytics to understand general website traffic patterns. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings, though this may affect some features of the site.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes secure socket layer (SSL) technology to encrypt data transmitted between your browser and our website.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it at any time. Simply email us at hello@yogaandpeace.com and we will process your request promptly, typically within 30 days.
