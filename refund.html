Refund & Cancellation Policy

Flexible, fair, and transparent policies designed to give you peace of mind.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Monthly Membership Cancellations

We do not believe in locking you into long-term contracts. You may cancel your monthly membership (Gentle Starter, Balanced Vitality, or Total Serenity) at any time.

  • How to cancel: You can cancel by emailing us at hello@yogaandpeace.com or by speaking to our front desk staff.
  • Notice period: Cancellations must be submitted at least 24 hours before your next scheduled billing date.
  • Final billing: If you cancel after your billing date has passed, you will retain access to your classes until the end of that current billing cycle. No further charges will be applied.

2. Refunds for Monthly Memberships

Because our memberships provide immediate access to our studio and online library, we do not offer prorated refunds for partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, your membership will remain active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Exception: If you experience a sudden medical emergency that prevents you from using our services, please contact us directly. We will review your situation on a case-by-case basis and may offer a credit or exception at our discretion.

3. Drop-In Classes and Workshops

For single drop-in classes or special weekend workshops:

  • Full refunds are available if you cancel at least 48 hours before the class start time.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of the class are non-refundable, but you may transfer your spot to a friend or family member at no extra charge.
  • If Yoga and Peace cancels a class due to instructor illness or facility issues, you will receive a full refund or a credit toward a future class, whichever you prefer.

4. Free Trial and Introductory Offers

Our complimentary introductory classes and free studio tours are exactly that—free. No payment information is required to claim them, and there is no obligation to purchase a membership afterward.

5. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please email our billing department at billing@yogaandpeace.com with your full name, membership email, and the reason for your request. We process all legitimate refund requests within 5 to 7 business days.
