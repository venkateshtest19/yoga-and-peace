Refund & Cancellation Policy

Flexible, fair, and transparent policies designed to give you complete peace of mind.

1. Our Philosophy on Cancellations

We believe that wellness should never feel like a burden. We do not lock you into long-term, restrictive contracts. Our goal is to keep you coming back because you love the community and the benefits, not because you are trapped in a billing cycle.

2. Monthly Membership Cancellations

You may cancel your monthly membership (Gentle Starter, Balanced Vitality, or Total Serenity) at any time.

  • How to cancel: You can cancel by emailing us at hello@yogaandpeace.com, calling our front desk, or speaking to our staff in person.
  • Notice period: Cancellations must be submitted at least 24 hours before your next scheduled billing date to avoid being charged for the upcoming month.
  • Final billing cycle: If you cancel after your billing date has passed, your membership will remain fully active until the end of that current billing cycle. No further charges will be applied.

3. Refunds for Monthly Memberships

Because our memberships provide immediate access to our studio, online library, and community resources, we do not offer prorated refunds for partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, your membership will remain active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Medical Exception: If you experience a sudden, severe medical emergency that prevents you from using our services for an extended period, please contact us directly. We will review your situation on a compassionate, case-by-case basis and may offer a credit or exception at our discretion.

4. Drop-In Classes and Special Workshops

For single drop-in classes or special weekend workshops:

  • Full refunds are available if you cancel at least 48 hours before the class start time.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of the class are non-refundable. However, you may transfer your spot to a friend or family member at no extra charge, provided you notify us in advance.
  • If Yoga and Peace cancels a class due to instructor illness, severe weather, or facility issues, you will receive a full refund or a credit toward a future class, whichever you prefer.

5. Free Trials and Introductory Offers

Our complimentary introductory classes and free studio tours are exactly that—free. No payment information is required to claim them, and there is absolutely no obligation to purchase a membership afterward.

6. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund under this policy, please email our billing department at hello@yogaandpeace.com with your full name, membership email, and the reason for your request. We process all legitimate refund requests within 5 to 7 business days, and the funds will be returned to your original method of payment.

If you have any questions about our refund or cancellation policies, we are here to help. Please reach out to us at:
